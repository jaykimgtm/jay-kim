About

I'm Jay Kim, a GTM recruiter based in Tokyo. At Salesforce I carried about 15 concurrent reqs from IC to VP across Japan and Korea, delivered 102% of my 2025 hiring target, and ranked #2 in Japan/Korea by external hires in fiscal Q2 2025. At Anthropic I joined the early Japan/Korea build-out, set up local recruiting operations and closed the first Partner Architect hire in Japan. I started in agency search at RGF, where I built an outreach automation tool that cut team outreach time by 30%. I work in Japanese, Korean and English, hold SHRM-SCP, and have built recruiting tools with code since long before it was cool.

Selected work

Same bar, better signal

Anthropic, 2026 Interview calibration

Challenge

HQ interview standards didn't fit local talent, and there was no Japan/Korea playbook. Language gaps and a different interview style lowered scores, and no one had the answer.

What I did

Added English-level questions at application; calibrated the presentation and interview rounds with the hiring manager and prepped candidates; built a semi-automated Slack pre-brief for interviewers new to Japanese candidates, backed by data and past successes.

Result

The first Partner Architect in Japan was hired within the H2 target.

Made open roles visible again

Anthropic, 2026 Referrals and hiring visibility

Challenge

As the company grew, Japan members lost sight of open roles and of their part in referring.

What I did

Asked members and groups for leads the day a role opened; ran monthly hiring updates at the office meeting with a top-down message from Japan and Korea leadership; ran a rapid sourcing session with the Korea team; built an internal open-roles tracker.

A Korea referral fast-track

Salesforce Market-specific process

Challenge

Korea hiring is referral-first (60–70% referral in my playbook).

What I did

Designed and launched a Korea-specific referral fast-track as initiator and organizer, with a team of five over three months.

Result

First-round interview rates up 12% and average time-to-hire down 5 days.

A daily competitor-hiring brief, built solo with zero budget

Salesforce Automation

Challenge

The team checked competitor job pages by hand every morning, and news was stale by the time it was shared.

What I did

Built a daily flow in three days: a scheduled Workspace trigger at 8:00 AM, Gemini research on Japan SaaS hiring across 20+ target companies, and an Apps Script that forwards the digest with duplicate detection.

Result

A fresh 24-hour competitor brief in every teammate's inbox with no manual work.

Data and AI for hiring manager alignment

Salesforce Analytics

Challenge

A department hiring hundreds of people was on target but worried about retention, with no clear pattern.

What I did

Analyzed past employees' resumes with an internal AI tool against performance rankings from the enablement team, after getting global AI council approval for sensitive data.

Result

The data was limited, but it debunked some hiring biases and opened a path to using AI for better hires.

How I work

Three habits with hiring managers and one playbook per market.

With hiring managers

Live pipeline sheet

What I do
A near-real-time Google Sheet synced from the ATS: status, stage and my notes
What changes
Managers check it between syncs; no "what's the update?" messages

Their comms style, not mine

What I do
Before the first req I ask for cadence, Slack or email, data-heavy or narrative
What changes
Faster feedback and higher response on candidate decisions

Data-backed suggestions

What I do
When the funnel stalls I bring the data, name the root cause and propose a fix: calibration, comp or interview design
What changes
Managers treat me as an advisor, not a service desk

Japan
